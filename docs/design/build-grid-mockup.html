Build grid, revised. Opens in dark mode: the grid stays a normal crossword (white squares, black blocks); only the app around it goes dark. Toggle with the Light/Dark button. Words that fit are always in the side panel and follow the answer you select (respecting letters already in place). Ctrl+Space jumps to the panel's search box. Right-click a square: clear or fill just that answer. Word breaks: type a split like 4,5 or 3-4 in the box beside the answer. Words that would leave a crossing answer impossible are not listed. This mockup carries the 40,000 most common entries; the app searches all 287,000.
